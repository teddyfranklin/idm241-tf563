UXID-241: MICROINTERACTIONS

Final Description

Form Locations

The category selection button is the second button down in the "category selection section." It is located on the left side of the form fields.

The start session button is the largest button of all the form fields. It is located at the bottom of all the buttons and is the largest button on the screen.

The home button is the "Wikipedia Art Reference" title in the purple header bar. It is located in the top left corner of every screen.

The image buttons are the Back, Reroll, Pause/Play, Next, and End buttons. They are located in the control bar directly above the image frame during a session.

Image Filter Checkbox Triggers

Hover on: The pointer moves over an image filter checkbox.

Hover off: The pointer moves off an image filter checkbox.

Click on: Clicking an unchecked checkbox turns that image filter on.

Click off: Clicking a checked checkbox turns that image filter off.

Home Button Triggers

Hover on: The pointer moves over the title, or the title receives keyboard focus.

Hover off: The pointer moves off the title, or keyboard focus leaves it.

Click on: The mouse button is held down on the title.

Click off: The mouse button is released on the title, or Enter or Space is pressed while it has focus.

Image Button Triggers

Hover on: The pointer moves over an enabled image button.

Hover off: The pointer moves off an image button.

Click on: The mouse button is held down on an enabled image button.

Click off: The mouse button is released, running that button's action.

Category Selection Rules

Hover

On

  • Changes background fill from #ffffff to #000000 over 400ms using an ease in and ease out transition.
  • Changes text and chevron from #000000 to #ffffff over 400 ms using an ease in and ease out transition.
  • While dropdown menu is open, hovering over a menu item changes its text from #ffffff to #969BFF over 400ms using an ease in and ease out transition.

Off

  • Changes background fill from #000000 to #ffffff over 400ms using an ease in and ease out transition.
  • Changes text and chevron from #ffffff to #000000 over 400 ms using an ease in and ease out transition.
  • While dropdown menu is open, moving the pointer off a menu item changes its text from #969BFF back to #ffffff over 400ms using an ease in and ease out transition.

Click

On

  • Clicking the initial dropdown menu button opens a dropdown menu with a selection of options for different image categories. The dropdown menu slides down over 400ms using an ease in and ease out transition and covers the content below it. The menu items are Quality images, Picture of the day, Featured picture candidates, and Featured pictures.
  • Clicking an option in the opened dropdown menu changes the content filter for the image search algorithm.
  • Opening the dropdown menu plays an animation that rotates the chevron 180 degrees over 400ms. It initially points up and then points down after the animation plays.

Off

  • Opening the dropdown menu plays an animation that rotates the chevron 180 degrees over 400ms. It initially points up and points down after the animation plays.
  • Clicking a menu option closes the dropdown menu.
  • The dropdown menu slides up over 400ms using an ease in and ease out transition as it closes.

Start Session Rules

Hover

On

  • Hovering over the start session button slowly fills the background color from #ffffff to #7675A0 in a gradient from left to right.
  • The text that is in front of the #7675A0 background turns from #000000 to #ffffff.

Off

  • The background fill reverses its gradient from right to left.
  • The text that is in front of the #ffffff background turns from #ffffff to #000000 as the background color changes behind it.

Click

On

  • Holding click on the start session button transforms the scale by .8
  • Releasing the click on the start session changes the scale from .8 back to 1.
  • When click is completed, the page changes from the dashboard to the image display page.

Image Filter Checkbox Rules

Hover

On

  • Hovering over an unchecked checkbox changes its background fill from #ffffff to #D1D0E8.
  • Hovering over a checked checkbox keeps its #8F8EAC fill. No hover color is applied on top of it.
  • The 16px by 16px checkbox keeps its 1px #000000 border in every state.

Off

  • Moving the pointer off an unchecked checkbox changes its background fill from #D1D0E8 back to #ffffff.
  • Moving the pointer off a checked checkbox leaves its #8F8EAC fill unchanged.

Click

On

  • Clicking an unchecked checkbox turns on its image filter. The filters are Black & White, Greyscale, and Grid Overlay.
  • A #8F8EAC square grows outward from the center of the checkbox, from 2px to 14px, over about 400ms until it fills the inside of the box.
  • Once the click is completed the checkbox stays filled with #8F8EAC to show the filter is active.

Off

  • Clicking a checked checkbox turns off its image filter.
  • The #8F8EAC square shrinks back into the center of the checkbox, from 14px to 2px, over about 400ms until the box is empty.
  • Once the click is completed the checkbox returns to its unchecked #ffffff fill.

Home Button Rules

Hover

  • Hovering over the title sweeps #000000 across the #ffffff text from left to right over 600ms until the whole title is black. Moving the pointer off reverses the sweep back to #ffffff.

Click

On

  • While the mouse button is held down, the title shrinks from 36px to 32px and shifts 17px to the right, staying solid #000000 so it looks pushed in.

Off

  • Releasing on the title during a session ends the session. The timer stops, any image still loading is cancelled, and the setup screen returns.
  • Releasing on the title while on the setup screen does not reset anything, so the typed category and filter choices stay. The page only scrolls back to the top.

Keyboard

  • Tabbing to the title shows the same #000000 sweep plus a #ffffff focus outline, and pressing Enter or Space works the same as a click.

Image Button Rules

Hover

  • Hovering over an enabled button fills it with #8F8EAC and turns its text #ffffff over 150ms. Moving the pointer off returns it to #ffffff.

Click

On

  • While the mouse button is held down, the button turns a darker #7675A0 with a slight inner shadow at the top, so it looks pressed in.

States

  • Disabled buttons ignore hover and press and show grey text. Back is disabled on the first image, Next on the last image, Reroll at 0, and Back, Next, and Reroll while an image is loading.
  • Pressing Pause stops the timer and relabels the button "Play". The button stays #8F8EAC for as long as the session is paused, and pressing Play resumes the timer and clears the fill.
  • Each Reroll press replaces the current image and lowers the count in the label, for example "Reroll (3)" to "Reroll (2)". At 0 the button becomes disabled.
